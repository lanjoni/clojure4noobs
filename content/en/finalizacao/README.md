# Wrap-up

Congratulations! You reached the end of this Clojure journey — and it is only the beginning of the adventure. Thank you for staying until the end. I hope this material helps you in your studies.

I am genuinely happy that you are reading this and that this content could help you. Everything here was written with care. None of it would exist without many people — and above all the community I love so much, He4rt Developers 💜!

I love every member and everyone who made these projects possible. The fair thing is to give back by contributing somehow, right?

Thank you to my friends and family, who have always been a source of inspiration and the pillars of everything I have aimed for. You are amazing.

Thank you to the Clojure contributors who keep the language growing. You are very special.

Clojure makes me happy, so it is only fair if it can make you happy too.

And you, right there behind the screen: the person writing this is proud of you, okay? You are amazing. Remember that anyone can learn something new, and anyone can contribute. As Aesop put it:

> *"No one is so great that they cannot learn, nor so small that they cannot teach"*

Thank you for everything.

*From the community, for the community. 💜*
